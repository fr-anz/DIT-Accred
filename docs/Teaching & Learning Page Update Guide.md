# Teaching & Learning Page Update Guide

Nov 17, 2026 · @Franz

This guide lists what to change on each section of https://dit-accred.vercel.app/teaching-learning so it matches the Area 2 draft (BSIT-Area-2-Teaching-and-Learning-WIP-10-19.docx). Each section has a priority: **Fix** means the page now shows something wrong or unsupported, **Add** means content from the draft is missing, and **Keep** means no change is needed now.

## 1. Governance summary (top strip)

**Fix / Add**

- Keep the CMO No. 25, s. 2015 and OBE references; they match the draft.
- Add a badge or card for the **2025 PICAB accreditation** (PCS Information and Computing Accreditation Board), noting that BSIT is the first PUP program to undergo this international accreditation.
- Keep the **Evidence-Based Curriculum Revision Lifecycle** title, but replace its current steps (benchmarking against DLSU, Mapúa and UP Diliman, then feedback, committee and board approval) with the five Curriculum Revision Steps from the draft's diagram: (1) Issuance of Memorandum; (2) Consultations with Different Stakeholders; (3) Drafting and Presentation of Revised Curriculum to the DCEC and the CCEC; (4) Presentation to the UCEC and University Academic Council; (5) Submission to BOR for Approval (BOR Resolution No. 2825 s. 2022). Link step 4 to the minutes excerpt of the University Curriculum Revision meeting.
