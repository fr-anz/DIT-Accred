## 5. Section Content: Continuous Quality Improvement (PDCA)

**Heading:** Continuous Quality Improvement

**Subtitle:** The Plan–Do–Check–Act Cycle

**Introduction (use as written):**

> The Department follows a systematic planning process aligned with the University's Vision, Mission, Goals and Objectives (VMGO). The same Plan–Do–Check–Act method behind PUP's ISO 9001 and ISO 21001 quality management systems guides how we plan, deliver, review and improve the BSIT program.

### The Four Stages

| Stage | Guiding question | What DIT does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan | What will we achieve, and how? | Set targets from the University's strategic priorities; revise the curriculum per CHED memoranda; prepare and update syllabi; commit targets in the DPCR and IPCRs |
| Do | Carry out the plan. | Deliver outcomes-based instruction; run internships with industry partners under MOAs; conduct research and extension projects; pursue international partnerships and mobility |
| Check | Did we meet our targets? | Report through EQAR, OPCR, DPCR and IPCR; hold mid-year and year-end assessments; undergo internal audits and accreditation visits; gather student and stakeholder feedback |
| Act | Improve and standardize. | Address accreditor recommendations; update the curriculum, syllabi and processes; adjust targets for the next cycle; adopt what worked as standard practice |

Each Do item matches an existing Department-Level Process subsection, so refer readers there rather than repeating it.

### Performance Reporting Tools (under Check)

| Tool | Full name | Level | Link to supply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EQAR | Electronic Quarterly Accomplishment Report | University | PUP eQAR |
| OPCR | Office Performance Commitment and Review | College (CCIS) | CCIS OPCR |
| DPCR | Department Performance Commitment and Review | Department (DIT) | DIT DPCR |
| IPCR | Individual Performance Commitment and Review | Faculty | DIT IPCR |

### ISO Note (2–3 sentences)

> PUP has held ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 21001:2018 certification since 2018. Both standards follow the Plan–Do–Check–Act method, with mid-year and year-end assessments running from delivery units up to top management. Read PUP's Quality Policy.
